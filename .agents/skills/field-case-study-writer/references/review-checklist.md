# Review Checklist

Score each applicable item as pass, revise, or missing evidence.

## Hiring clarity

- The first screen identifies Nick as a Design Engineer.
- The project proves a capability relevant to a frontend-leaning Design Engineer role.
- The main argument is visible without reading the entire page.
- The project does not make Nick appear to be only a product designer, frontend developer, visual designer, or generic web designer.

## Context

- The user or customer is identifiable.
- The problem is concrete.
- The reason the problem mattered is clear.
- Company history and process detail do not overwhelm the project.

## Ownership

- `I` and `we` are used accurately.
- Nick's role is distinguishable from team contributions.
- The page says what Nick designed, built, defined, tested, or influenced.
- Scope is not inflated.

## Judgment

- The page includes consequential decisions rather than a chronological process dump.
- Each important decision has a reason.
- Meaningful tradeoffs or constraints are visible.
- The page shows product judgment, not only visual execution.

## Implementation proof

- The page includes production or buildable evidence.
- Technical details connect to product quality.
- States, responsiveness, accessibility, testing, performance, or edge cases appear where relevant.
- The page does not rely only on polished screenshots.

## Evidence and outcomes

- Claims are supported by metrics, adoption, tests, artifacts, or qualified feedback.
- Proxy outcomes are labeled honestly.
- Missing metrics are not invented.
- The reflection identifies what should be measured next when appropriate.

## Writing quality

- Each concept uses a consistent name.
- Paragraphs are focused.
- Active voice is used where it clarifies ownership.
- Concrete verbs replace inflated language.
- Technical terminology is accurate.
- The draft avoids duplicated points and unnecessary transitions.
- The page is easy to scan.

## Theme restraint

- Operational language appears mainly in labels and metadata.
- Standard words are used when they are clearer.
- There is no fake military lore, classified language, tactical cosplay, or aggressive metaphor.
- The page feels like a technical briefing, not entertainment UI.
- Atmosphere supports the work instead of competing with it.

## Repository quality

- Existing content conventions are preserved.
- Frontmatter and MDX remain valid.
- Links and imports remain intact.
- Formatting, linting, type checking, and content validation pass when available.
- No unrelated files or dependencies were added.
